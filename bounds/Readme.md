# List of bounds:

- **Binary evaporation** - Constraints from the disruption of Gaia wide binaries by EDOs over 10 Gyr (arXiv 2209.08100).

- **Binary survival** - Constraints from the survival of Gaia wide binaries against perturbations by EDOs (arXiv 2404.18099).

- **CMB accretion** - Constraints from baryonic accretion onto EDOs altering the recombination history and CMB (arXiv 2403.13072).

- **Dynamical heating** - Constraints from the gravitational heating of stars in ultrafaint dwarf galaxies by EDOs (arXiv 2404.01378).

- **EROS-2** - Microlensing constraints from EROS-2 observations of stars in the Magellanic Clouds (arXiv 2002.08962).

- **Icarus** - Constraints from the effect of EDOs on caustic crossings of highly magnified stars such as Icarus (arXiv 2511.20761).

- **LVK** - Gravitational-wave constraints on mergers of EDO binaries formed in the early Universe (arXiv 2205.15396).

- **OGLE-IV** - Microlensing constraints from OGLE-IV observations toward the Galactic bulge (arXiv 2002.08962).

- **SN lensing DES** - Supernova microlensing constraints using the DES Type Ia supernova sample (arXiv 2609.04308).

- **SN lensing LSST** - Forecasted supernova microlensing constraints for the LSST Type Ia supernova sample (arXiv 2609.04308).

- **Subaru-HSC** - Microlensing constraints from Subaru-HSC observations of M31 including finite source effects (arXiv 2007.12697).
